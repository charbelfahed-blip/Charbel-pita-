The secrets to full pockets

330–350°C

Target the stone, not just the oven air. Check with an infrared thermometer.

  1. Roll each disk to even thickness, including the rim.
  2. Keep dough covered. A dry skin or a pinhole leaks steam.
  3. Let gluten relax before rolling; avoid forcing the shape.
  4. Wait for a steady floor temperature between batches.
  5. Cover baked pita promptly to keep it soft.

04 · If a pita doesn't puff

◉ Small bubbles, no balloon

Look for an uneven disk, tears, dry patches, insufficient floor heat or dough that's springing back.

◉ Brown bottom, no pocket

Stone or radiant heat may be too intense. Let it settle and test another disk.

◉ Balloons, then collapses

That is normal after baking. Remove once fully puffed and lightly golden; cool on a towel-lined rack.
